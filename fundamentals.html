Weapon Knowledge

  • Sidearms - are the secondary weapons that Agents can equip. These are low-cost weapons, and cover a variety of types of small and lightweight weapons i.e. Machine pistols, sawed-off shotguns, revolvers, etc.
  • SMGs - are fully-automatic firearms that have high rates of fire in exchange for low damage per shot. Both SMGs have fully-automatic modes, but the Stinger has an additional burst-fire mode when aiming down sights.
  • Shotguns - are weapons that fire off a number of pellets around a circular spread. These weapons have high close-range damage, in exchange for a somewhat slow reload time and low accuracy. The Bucky has an additional firing mode that fires off a canister of pellets that explodes in mid-air, increasing its accuracy at mid-range.
  • Rifles - are weapons that are fully-automatic, with the exceptions of the Guardian, which is semi-auto, and the Bulldog, which has an additional burst-fire mode.
  • Sniper rifles - are semi-automatic firearms that can deal high damage from a long range, in exchange for low accuracy when not aiming down sights and standing still. Every sniper rifle has a unique scope that can zoom in to provide extra accuracy.

Beginner tips for each class

Sidearms

Keep your sidearm as a backup. Switch to it quickly when your primary is empty or when you need to move fast.

SMGs

Fire in short bursts at medium range. Controlling the spray matters more than raw damage per bullet.

Shotguns

Use them close to a corner or choke point, where the pellets are most likely to land on an enemy.

Rifles

Learn the recoil pattern of your main rifle in the range and pull your crosshair back against it as you fire.

Sniper rifles

Scope in and stand still for accuracy. Moving while unscoped makes your shots much less reliable.

Machine guns

Ares and Odin are heavy and slow to move with. Use them to hold a long lane rather than to push into one.

Aiming drills

Spend about ten minutes warming up before each session. These two drills cover the basics.

Training Range

Open the practice range from the game's main menu and shoot at targets at different distances. Stand still first to learn how accurate each weapon's first bullet is, then strafe and shoot to practise stopping before you fire.

Death Match

Death Match is a free-for-all mode with quick respawns, so you get many fights in a short time. Use it to warm up with the weapons and maps you plan to play, and focus on landing your first bullet rather than chasing kills.
