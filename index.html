Valorant Road Map

VALORANT Road Map is an easy-to-learn website intended for beginners who want to strengthen the basics of their game. The site contains tutorials and guides for aim, movement, positioning, and game sense, so you always know what to work on and how.

Beginner Friendly

Built for beginners, this roadmap helps players improve the skills that matter most when starting VALORANT. Learn how to aim more consistently, move and position yourself properly, understand your agent's abilities, recognize common enemy positions, and make better decisions during rounds. By following each step, beginners can build confidence, avoid common mistakes, and know exactly what to practice next.
